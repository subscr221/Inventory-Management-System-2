# Story 9.5: Statutory Return Clocks and Closure Gate

Status: review

<!-- Note: Validation is optional. Run validate-create-story for quality check before dev-story. -->

## Story

As a compliance officer,
I want Rule 45 challans with one-year and three-year return clocks from the challan date, deemed-supply alerts, ITC-04 data, an aging report, and a closure gate that blocks on non-zero custody,
so that job-work statutory obligations are met and no order closes with unaccounted customer material.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Return clocks start from challan date (FR-AC-11).** Given customer material received on a Rule 45 challan, when the challan is recorded (Story 9.2, `jobwork_material_receipt.challan_date`), then a one-year (inputs) and three-year (capital goods) return-clock exposure is computed from that date and is visible on the order.
2. **Reconciliation closes clock exposure (FR-AC-11).** Given an open return clock, when processed output or unconsumed material is returned or dispatched and reconciled against the challan quantity, then the clock exposure for the reconciled quantity is closed and the aging report reflects the reduced exposure.
3. **Breach-window alert (FR-JW-14).** Given a return clock entering its breach window - configurable lead times per challan class, defaulting to 90 and 30 days before expiry - when a lead-time threshold is crossed, then a deemed-supply warning alert naming the order, challan, and expiry date is delivered through the Story 1.11 notification foundation to the job-work coordinator and the compliance officer.
4. **Escalation on inaction (FR-JW-14).** Given a breach-window alert not actioned, when the configured escalation interval elapses without the exposure being cleared, then the alert escalates through Story 1.11 to the next tier resolved from the DOA registry (FR-DOA-01) - no alert expires silently.
5. **Deemed supply on expiry (FR-AC-11).** Given a return clock that expires with unreconciled quantity, when the one-year or three-year limit passes, then the breached quantity is flagged as a deemed supply on the order, a deemed-supply record is raised into the ITC-04 data set, and an escalation is sent through Story 1.11 to the compliance officer and site head.
6. **ITC-04 and aging reports (FR-AC-11, FR-JW-14).** Given job-work movements in a period, when ITC-04 reporting is run, then ITC-04 data - including any deemed-supply records - and a job-work aging report are produced.
7. **Physical-verification variance reconciliation (FR-JW-13).** Given customer stock included in a physical verification count, when the count records a variance against the custody ledger (via the Story 2.6 count workflow), then the variance is reconciled on the next custody statement for that customer and order, attributed to the verifying user.
8. **Closure gate blocks on non-zero custody (FR-JW-15, FR-AC-11).** Given a service order submitted for closure, when the custody ledger balance is non-zero, then closure is blocked with `error_code: "CUSTODY_NOT_ZERO"` until the ledger is reconciled to zero.
