## Epic Summary

Epic 9 delivers job-work services: customer-owned material received into segregated stock, consumed against customer-supplied kit BOMs through a per-order custody ledger, process loss and offcut handled distinctly, FG dispatch gated by QC, statutory Rule 45 return clocks running from the challan date, and closure blocked while the custody ledger is non-zero.

## What Went Well

- **Custody ledger spine held.** The single append-only `custody_ledger_entry` projection introduced in Story 9.3 absorbed every downstream concern - loss, offcut capture, dispatch, return clocks, disposal, acquisition - without rework.
- **Noise-floor elimination paid for itself.** Clearing the carried 28-failure floor surfaced a real bug (silent allocation leak on rejected transfers) that had been masked as "known noise" for weeks.
- **Same-day decision resolution.** Party-mode round-tables closed open questions live (Stories 9.1, 9.5, 9.6 x2, 9.7, 9.8) instead of blocking on PO availability - zero stories stalled waiting on an answer.
- **Review process scaled.** Chunked adversarial review (Groups/chunks A-E) handled the epic's more complex stories (9.6, 9.7) without losing rigor, and each chunk's decisions were tracked and closed explicitly rather than left open.

## What Didn't Go Well

- **Mid-epic domain reversal.** Story 9.6's offcut billing model was built, reviewed, and then reversed by an approved sprint-change-proposal once the commercial reality (offcut as a separately-contracted asset under CGST Section 143, not an ERP billing line) was understood correctly. This is the second consecutive epic with a mid-flight domain-model correction.
- **Recurring hold-bypass bug class.** The same shape of defect - a QC/custody hold on a lot being bypassable through some new write path - was found and individually patched in Stories 8.3, 8.4, 8.5, 8.8, and again in 9.4, before 9.4 finally consolidated the check into a shared `dispatchGateBlockedLots` helper. Four of those five were found by review, not design.
- **A known HIGH shipped deferred.** Story 9.8 closed the signature-forgery gap on the disposal approval path but explicitly left the same gap open on the revaluation route ("its taking too much of time"), tracked as deferred-work 9.8-2. This is a financial-approval-adjacent gap, not a cosmetic one.
- **Loose commit hygiene.** Several stories (9.5, 9.6, 9.7) accumulated multiple review rounds uncommitted before landing under generic messages ("commit"), making it harder to trace which patch closed which finding after the fact.
- **Unverified coverage gap.** Whether offcut-class stock is correctly included in cycle-count / physical-verification reconciliation was flagged as open as of 2026-09-06; not confirmed closed by the 9.7 chunk-D completion.

## Key Takeaways

1. The custody-ledger-as-spine design (Story 9.3) was the right call - every subsequent story extended it without rework, in contrast to the offcut billing model, which had to be reversed.
2. Recurring bug classes (hold-bypass, x5) are cheaper to consolidate into a shared guard early than to keep re-discovering per story; do this proactively next epic rather than after the fifth instance.
3. Deferring a known HIGH under time pressure is sometimes the right call, but it must convert into a tracked, prioritized blocker for the next unit of work - not silently ride along. That conversion happened here via this retrospective.
