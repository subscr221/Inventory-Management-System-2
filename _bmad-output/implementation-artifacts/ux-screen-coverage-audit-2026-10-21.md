# UX Screen Coverage Audit - 2026-10-21

This audit compares every screen the final UX run (the 2026-09-23 experience spine, its design spec and its key mockups) expects against what the edge PWA actually renders, what the server bootstrap advertises, and what the story records claim. Each row of the matrix and each confirmed gap carries file and line evidence. Where three independent review lenses (code, spec, user flow) examined a gap, their verdicts are summarised so the owner can see where the lenses agreed and where they disagreed.

As of 2026-10-21, master working tree (HEAD 4f37d32, `git status` clean, no stash). The edge PWA under `edge/` is the only front end in the repository; `src/` contains no `.html`, `.ejs` or `.tsx`, so any screen a story hands to "the central app" or "the edge team" has nowhere else to live.

The nine high-severity gaps share one shape: Epics 3, 4, 7 and 8 closed their stories as backend-only, each with a sentence handing the screen to "the edge team" or "the central app", and no edge story was ever created. The consequence is that the gate officer, weighbridge operator, storekeeper (for receiving and putaway), picker, dispatch clerk, unloading supervisor, QC inspector, QC head, department head approver and maintenance supervisor each have no edge surface for their primary job, even though the server APIs for those jobs exist and the pilot roster provisions accounts for every one of those roles.

## 2. Method and evidence conventions

Three lenses examined every candidate gap independently: a code lens (does the route, component, nav entry and server advertisement exist in the committed tree), a spec lens (does the final spine require the screen, or was it parked, folded or superseded), and a user-flow lens (can the persona complete the key flow on each device class through any existing screen). A gap is confirmed when at least two lenses could not refute it. Severity is reported as the majority verdict of the three lenses, with the original rating and any dissent noted.

Citations use the forms in the matrix legend below. Line numbers were read from the working tree on the audit date.
