- (decision) Ergonomic leads over modern when the two conflict (user, party round 1).
- (decision) Role priority for UX work: gate first, then stores, then approvers, then finance.
- (decision) Gate officer devices: phone plus rugged scanner; some have a rugged tablet. Gate UI must work across phone, scanner (hardware trigger/keyboard-wedge) and tablet.
- (decision) Gate capture supports BOTH scan-to-fill (challan QR / number-plate capture) AND manual entry with large targets; manual is the always-available fallback.
- (decision) Two density modes under one token set: touch-roomy for gate and stores, compact for approvers and finance.
- (assumption) Frontline touch targets at least 56px with primary action full-width in bottom third; desk surfaces keep 44px floor (Sally proposal, Murat testable; user not yet confirmed numbers).
- (event) Amelia grep found no dedicated gate-entry screen in edge/src/components; backend has weighbridge.ts and gate_dwell_metric; gate passes are Epic 20 Phase 2. Unverified against dashboard screens.
- (decision) Supplier paperwork mix at gate: about 50% carry QR/barcode, about 50% handwritten. Both paths are first-class, neither is an edge case.
- (decision) Gate entry core fields (priority): challan photo, challan reference, vehicle number, supplier. Driver, weight etc. are secondary/optional.
- (decision) Rugged scanner is Android-based (model and screen size unknown). Design small-first so the scanner screen is the tightest canvas; tablet may go two-column.
- (assumption) Plate OCR is best-effort on-device with mandatory officer confirmation; never auto-submit. Scan-to-fill resolves challan QR against locally synced PO/ASN rows offline.
- (decision) Gate entry step order: CHALLAN FIRST (scan QR/barcode or type the reference), THEN photo. User overruled both photo-first (Victor/Ravi) and scan-only (Winston).
- (decision) Unlisted supplier allowed at gate: fuzzy pick from synced vendor master, with an Unlisted supplier option that flags the entry for stores to match later. The truck is never blocked at the gate for an unknown supplier.

- (decision) Storekeeper job (user): accepts material into the store, takes photographs, assigns a rack, ensures traceability, issues items, and puts returned items back on the shelf. Stores is the second priority surface after gate.
- (decision) Storekeeper devices: rugged scanner, tablet and desktop, depending on where they are. Stores surfaces must work across all three.
- (decision) Racks/bins carry barcode or QR labels today; stores has a label printer to tag received material.
- (decision) Stores home = task inbox (arrived trucks awaiting acceptance, pending issues, returns) with an always-on-top Scan anything bar (scan rack, item or challan and the app offers the matching action).
- (decision) Rack assignment: system suggests the rack (directed putaway).
- (assumption) Storekeeper can override the suggested rack in one tap (existing directed-putaway override that teaches the system); user said system suggests, override not explicitly confirmed.
- (assumption) Gate entry hands off to stores inbox as an arrived-awaiting-acceptance item carrying challan, supplier and PO lines (no retyping).
- (override) Density follows device input, not role: coarse pointer or small viewport = touch-roomy (>=56px targets), fine pointer = compact. Supersedes the earlier role-based density split (gate/stores roomy, approvers/finance compact).
- (assumption) Gate surface is always touch-roomy regardless of device (Sally guard); user approved density-by-input but did not explicitly rule on this floor.
- (decision) Label printing is non-blocking: GRN/acceptance completes, label goes to a print queue with a Reprint action on the item. Every received lot gets a printed QR resolving to its GRN, PO line and rack.
- (decision) Excess qty: design supports both return-with-vehicle and hold-for-buyer. PILOT: UI caps accepted qty at PO + over_receipt_tolerance_pct and records the remainder as 'Excess - returned with vehicle' (reason code); only accepted qty is posted, so no backend change and no whole-line RECEIPT_TOLERANCE_EXCEEDED reject. POST-PILOT: 'Hold for buyer decision' routes excess to quarantine and creates an approval item.
- (decision) Receiving reason codes (fixed, coded, grouped so <=4 tiles per screen): SHORT = supplier short-shipped, part delivery - balance to follow, count mismatch with challan; DAMAGED = transit damage, packing damage, rust or corrosion; REJECTED = wrong item or spec (to QC); OTHER = mandatory photo plus one line. Labels site-configurable later.
- (decision) Per GRN line record Received = Good + Damaged + Rejected. Good goes to suggested rack; damaged and rejected go to a quarantine bay, never the shelf. Shared state pattern: problem stock is not on shelf until resolved.
- (assumption) Pilot feasibility of splitting one PO line across rack and quarantine locations in one GRN is unverified (Amelia to check); if unsupported, it becomes a small backend story.
- (event) Backend check (PARTIAL): split works today via two POST /api/v1/grn-lines calls on the same grn_id (good qty to rack target_location_id, damaged qty to quarantine location), optional own lot + PUT /lots/:id/quality-hold to lock it in quarantine. Missing: no damaged/rejected qty field and no reason code on a grn line; damaged line shows status posted with putaway ready, not held. Tolerance is CUMULATIVE per PO line (all non-rejected lines + legacy received vs ordered x (1+pct)), compliance/receiving.ts:629-645.
- (change) Pilot receiving pattern refined: UI computes remaining band = ordered x (1+tolerance) - already received, and caps posted qty (good + damaged kept) to it; the rest is excess returned with vehicle and not posted. Damaged kept for debit note posts as a second grn line to a quarantine location with quality hold; damaged sent back on the truck is not posted. Wrong item or spec is never posted against the PO line.
- (assumption) Backend story needed post-pilot (or pre-pilot if reason reporting is required): condition (good/damaged/rejected) and reason_code on grn lines so reasons are stored in the event, not only in the UI.
- (decision) Receiving reason reporting is REQUIRED AT PILOT. Pre-pilot backend story: add condition (good/damaged/rejected) and reason_code (fixed coded list) to grn lines and the goods.received event, with damaged/rejected lines routed to quarantine with quality hold. Needs a story via bmad-create-story or bmad-correct-course (adds pilot scope).
- (override) Damage is NOT decided by gate officer or storekeeper. Damage is usually found later when packaging is opened, not at the gate. Only QC and finance decide, after assessment and inspection. Supersedes the storekeeper-recorded Good/Damaged/Rejected split and the storekeeper-picked damage reasons.
- (decision) Storekeeper and gate only REPORT: at receipt they may note visible external damage (observation + photo, not a verdict); at any later time (e.g. unpacking) anyone handling stock can 'Report damage' by scanning the lot/item. Reporting places the stock on hold (not issuable) and routes it to QC for inspection; QC then finance decide the outcome.
- (event) Backend has a QC module: /api/v1/qc/tasks/:id/observations, /results, /inspection-completion, /split, /disposition (lot dispositions accept|reject, plus conditional_release, DOA-governed), lot quality_hold placed/cleared. No edge UI for QC found. Unverified: ad-hoc QC task creation from a damage report, and any finance commercial outcome (debit note / return to supplier / write-off) endpoint.
- (assumption) The pre-pilot backend story reshapes: 'Report damage' = lot quality hold + QC task carrying reason code and photo, reusing the QC module (observations, split, disposition); finance commercial decision surface still to be designed.
- (override) ALL received items land first in a separate INSPECTION BAY (not directly on the suggested rack). Rack assignment/putaway to the suggested rack happens after inspection clearance. Supersedes the stores mock's rack-at-acceptance step.
- (decision) At receipt, items with visible damage are marked 'Inspect on opening - damage suspected' or 'Inspect on opening - damage observed' (a flag for the inspector, not a verdict).
- (decision) Damage outcome requires CONCURRENCE of both QC and finance. They may decide together or one after the other depending on the situation; the outcome is final only when both have concurred.
- (decision) Anyone in the chain can report damage, including shop-floor machine operators (e.g. damaged component, or dead-on-arrival electronic part found after work starts). Report damage is a universal action, not a stores-only screen.
- (decision) If QC and finance disagree on a damage outcome, the case escalates to the CEO for the final decision.
- (decision) Hold scope: localised damage holds only the reported units, nothing elsewhere. If it is a quality issue, there is an option to hold ALL remaining stock of that lot everywhere (store, inspection bay, shop floor).
- (decision) Shop-floor machine operators report damage from a shared terminal at the machine (no personal device).
- (decision) Report damage + Request replacement is one flow, IN PILOT SCOPE (replacement becomes an indent/issue request so the line is not stopped).
- (decision) Devices: finance on desk; QC on both tablet (at the inspection bay) and desk.
