Legend - decisions and assumptions

  1. DECISION Every role on every device: picker, dispatch clerk and unloading supervisor each work on Handheld, Tablet, Desktop and Desktop touch. The role decides what is shown; the device class decides how. There are no shared machine terminals.
  2. DECISION Handheld (scanner or phone, finger): touch-roomy, primary 56px pinned at the bottom, secondary 48px, bottom nav (Tasks, Scan, Stock, More), one column; a task opens as its own page with a back button.
  3. DECISION Tablet (finger): touch-roomy, icon rail on the left, task list and task detail side by side.
  4. DECISION Desktop (mouse): compact 30-36px controls, dark sidebar #1B2230, top bar with search, site switcher CMF-ALIGARH and + New, list and detail side by side.
  5. DECISION Desktop touch (touchscreen PC): the desktop layout with touch-roomy controls.
  6. DECISION Scanning: the "Scanner ready" hardware indicator shows only on Handheld. Every scan also offers the camera (Handheld and Tablet) and always "Type it"; Desktop shows "USB scanner or type".
  7. DECISION Switching device keeps the current step (one shared state).
  8. DECISION Accent A Ledger Blue #1E6BC6 with its five-token family, same as stores.
  9. DECISION Pilot roles grouped into the Warehouse floor tasks surface: warehouse_operator (picker), dispatch_clerk and unloading_supervisor exceptions.
  10. DECISION Scan anything bar plus task inbox, the stores home pattern, reused here as the shared floor pattern.
  11. DECISION Racks and bins carry barcode or QR labels, so the bin scan confirms the picker is at the right place.
  12. DECISION Problem stock is not issuable: a lot on QC hold is blocked at scan with "On hold - awaiting QC".
  13. DECISION Report damage is universal: the short-pick reason "Damaged" places the units on hold and sends them to QC. The picker reports and QC and finance decide.
  14. DECISION Excess at receipt (pilot): accept up to ordered x (1 + tolerance) minus already received; the rest is "Excess - returned with vehicle" and not posted. "Hold for buyer decision" is post-pilot. 5% is a mock value.
  15. DECISION Unlisted supplier is allowed at the gate and flagged for matching to the vendor master later. The truck is never blocked.
  16. DECISION Offline-first: every capture shows "Captured - pending sync" until synced.
  17. ASSUMPTION Picking, packing and dispatch layouts were not elicited with the user. They are drafted from roles and epics and need review.
  18. ASSUMPTION System-directed bin sequence, one line at a time: scan bin, then scan lot, then confirm qty. The system picks the lot (FEFO/FIFO).
  19. ASSUMPTION Short-pick reasons: Bin empty; Less in bin than shown; Damaged - report; Other (one line required).
  20. ASSUMPTION Pack flow: scan each picked lot into the package, weight is required (scale read or typed), then "Generate shipping documents" (invoice with IRN, e-way bill, packing list).
  21. ASSUMPTION Missing IRN blocks dispatch. Billing is notified automatically; the clerk can park the package at bay D-02 and check again. The IRN check needs the network.
  22. ASSUMPTION Unloading exceptions arrive from gate and unloading. Each has one resolve action: match to PO, accept within band and return excess, or match to vendor. Unresolved cases go to procurement (Kavita Singh).
  23. ASSUMPTION After an exception is resolved, the delivery moves to the stores inbox for acceptance into the inspection bay.
  24. ASSUMPTION Typical desk use: the unloading supervisor resolves exceptions at a desk PC, and the dispatch clerk packs at a pack-station PC with a USB scanner (keyboard-wedge, types into the focused scan field). These are examples, not device limits.
  25. ASSUMPTION Tablet camera is the tablet's scan method; "Camera" is simulated here as a successful scan of the expected label.
