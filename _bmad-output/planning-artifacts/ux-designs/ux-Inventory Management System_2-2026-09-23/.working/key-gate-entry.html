Legend - decisions rendered

  1. DECISION Ergonomic over modern; small-first, scanner is the tightest canvas; tablet goes two-column.
  2. DECISION Step order: challan first, then photo, then vehicle, then supplier, then submit.
  3. DECISION Core fields only: challan reference, challan photo, vehicle number, supplier. Driver, weight, remarks sit under "More details (optional)".
  4. DECISION QR and handwritten challans are both first-class: Scan challan, or Type challan number.
  5. DECISION Scanner is always armed; hardware trigger works on any step ("Scanner ready").
  6. ASSUMPTION QR resolves offline against synced PO/ASN and pre-fills supplier via a Matched chip the officer accepts.
  7. ASSUMPTION "Read from photo" for the plate is best-effort; it pre-fills and must be confirmed. Never auto-submits.
  8. DECISION Unlisted supplier allowed; flagged "Stores will match this later". Truck never blocked.
  9. DECISION Submit shows "Captured - pending sync" and works with no network.
  10. ASSUMPTION Touch targets at least 56px; primary action full-width, pinned in the bottom third. Numbers not yet confirmed by user.
  11. DECISION Touch-roomy density only; compact (desk) mode not shown.
  12. DECISION No scan toast. The green "Matched: PO-..." card is the only visible scan feedback; screen readers still hear it via a hidden live region.
  13. DECISION "Next: photo" stays disabled until the challan is resolved: match Accepted, or "Not this" then a typed number, or a number typed manually.
  14. DECISION After a scan, the challan step is compact: "Scan again" and "Type challan number" share one secondary row, so typing stays reachable without scrolling on the 360 x 640 scanner.
  15. DECISION Typeface is Lato (loaded locally in production), falling back to Segoe UI, Roboto, system-ui.
  16. Colors are the existing app tokens only; palette is not decided.
