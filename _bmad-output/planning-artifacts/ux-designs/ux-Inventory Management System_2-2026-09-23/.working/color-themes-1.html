Reading the chips
  • Every candidate's primary button (white on accent) passes 4.5:1. Blue, Teal and Terracotta were darkened from their first picks to get there; the hex notes are in the page source.
  • The raw accent is too dark on the #1B2230 sidebar for Teal, Indigo and Terracotta (under 3:1), and Blue only just reaches 3.01. So the sidebar active bar and icon use a derived lighter token, --accent-on-dark, which passes 4.5:1 for all four. This adds a fifth per-site token.
  • Fixed neutrals: sidebar #1B2230, page #F4F6F9, surface #FFFFFF, border #DDE2E9, text #1C2430, muted text #566173. Status colours are fixed and never take the accent.
