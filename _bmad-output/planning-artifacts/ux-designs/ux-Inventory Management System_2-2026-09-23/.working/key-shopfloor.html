Shop floor - shared machine terminal and floor supervisor indents

One shared state across both frames. The terminal is mounted at CNC-02 and used by whoever is at the machine: each report asks for a badge scan or PIN, is signed with that person, then the terminal signs out and returns to idle. A replacement requested at the terminal shows up on the floor supervisor's phone under "Raised at CNC-02 terminal". Tap inside the frames or use Demo controls.

Legend - decisions rendered

  1. DECISION Shop-floor machine operators report damage from a shared terminal at the machine, with no personal device.
  2. DECISION Report damage is universal: anyone in the chain can report, including operators who find a damaged component or a dead-on-arrival electronic part after work starts.
  3. DECISION The operator only reports. Reporting puts the reported units on hold (not issuable) and routes them to QC. QC and finance decide the outcome together; if they disagree, the CEO decides.
  4. DECISION Hold scope: a report holds only the reported units. A lot-wide hold (store, inspection bay, shop floor) is an option for a quality issue.
  5. DECISION Report damage + Request replacement is one flow, in pilot scope. The replacement becomes an indent for the same item and qty so the line is not stopped.
  6. DECISION Density follows input: terminal and phone are touch-roomy (primary 56px pinned at the bottom, secondary 48px, keypad keys 72px).
  7. DECISION Accent A Ledger Blue #1E6BC6 token family; dark bar #1B2230; phone keeps the touch bottom nav.
  8. DECISION Offline-first: every capture shows "Captured - pending sync" until it syncs.
  9. ASSUMPTION Per-report sign-in: tapping an action asks "Scan your ID badge or enter PIN", the report is signed with that person, then the terminal signs out ("Signed out - terminal ready"). Auto return after 15 s on the confirmation, and after 60 s with no taps.
  10. ASSUMPTION "Suspect whole lot" is a REQUEST only; the QC head decides the lot-wide hold. The reporter's own hold is always local.
  11. ASSUMPTION Operators are identified by badge ID (OP-2217) because no operator name is in the pilot roster; production shows the badge holder's name.
  12. ASSUMPTION Damage reasons for use-time damage: Dead on arrival - electronic; Damaged component; Wrong item or spec; Other (photo + one line required). Photo optional for the first three.
  13. ASSUMPTION "Material issued to this machine" lists open issues to CNC-02 from the last 7 days; qty affected is capped at the qty issued.
  14. ASSUMPTION The replacement indent is urgent, needed today, charged to the machine's cost centre, and goes through normal indent approval.
  15. ASSUMPTION Report machine fault is a stub (raises a maintenance request); the maintenance flow is not elicited.
  16. ASSUMPTION Quick indent mirrors edge IndentCapture fields. Picking an item fills SKU, category and UOM; picking the cost centre fills department and business stream; unit price and reason sit under "More details (optional)". The timer shows the 90-second UJ-IND-01 target.
  17. ASSUMPTION My requests shows three statuses: Pending approval, Approved, Issued. Rejected and part-issued are not drawn.
