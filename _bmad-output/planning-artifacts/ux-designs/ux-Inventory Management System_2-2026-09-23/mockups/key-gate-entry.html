Gate entry - key screen mock

One gate-entry flow in one frame. Pick a device class; the current step and everything typed so far stay put when you switch. Tap inside the frame or use Demo controls. "Trucks" / "Trucks on site" opens the weighbridge: weigh in (gross), unloading, weigh out (tare), net against the PO tolerance.

Legend - decisions rendered

  1. DECISION Every role on every device. Role decides what is shown; the device class decides how. No shared machine terminals.
  2. DECISION Handheld (scanner or phone): touch-roomy, single column, bottom nav; primary action full width pinned in the bottom third.
  3. DECISION Tablet: touch-roomy, icon rail on the left, step list beside the form.
  4. DECISION Desktop: gate is compact like any desktop (34px controls), dark sidebar with Gate active, top bar with search, CMF-ALIGARH site switcher and + New, step list left of the form.
  5. DECISION Desktop touch: desktop layout and dark sidebar, touch-roomy density.
  6. DECISION Step order: challan first, then photo, then vehicle, then supplier, then submit.
  7. DECISION Core fields only: challan reference, challan photo, vehicle number, supplier. Driver, challan weight, remarks sit under "More details (optional)". Weighbridge weights are taken in Trucks on site, not in the entry form.
  8. DECISION QR and handwritten challans are both first-class: scan the challan, or type the challan number. Typing is always offered on every device.
  9. DECISION Scanner is always armed on every step. "Scanner ready" shows on Handheld only; Handheld and Tablet also scan with the camera; Desktop shows "USB scanner or type".
  10. ASSUMPTION QR resolves offline against synced PO/ASN and pre-fills supplier via a Matched card the officer accepts.
  11. ASSUMPTION "Read from photo" for the plate is best-effort; it pre-fills and must be confirmed. Never auto-submits.
  12. DECISION Unlisted supplier allowed; flagged "Stores will match this later". Truck never blocked.
  13. DECISION Submit shows "Captured - pending sync" and works with no network.
  14. DECISION No scan toast. The green Matched card is the only visible scan feedback; screen readers hear it via a live region.
  15. DECISION "Next: photo" stays disabled until the challan is resolved: match accepted, or "Not this" then a typed number, or a number typed manually.
  16. DECISION After a scan the challan step is compact: "Scan again" and "Type challan number" share one row, so typing stays reachable without scrolling on Handheld.
  17. DECISION Colours: theme A Ledger Blue (#1E6BC6) for CMF-ALIGARH, dark sidebar #1B2230, lighter accent-on-dark for the active nav marker.
  18. DECISION Typeface is Lato (loaded locally in production), falling back to Segoe UI, Roboto, system-ui.
  19. ASSUMPTION Touch sizes: primary at least 56px, secondary at least 48px. Numbers not yet confirmed by the user.
  20. ASSUMPTION On Desktop the challan photo comes from "Upload photo" or "Use webcam".
  21. DECISION Weighbridge capture belongs in the gate surface (memlog Q9). Ramesh Yadav holds both roles, gate_officer and weighbridge_operator (the only role allowed to write weighments in src/api/v1/weighbridge.ts).
  22. DECISION Every weighment is bound to an open gate entry (binding token = the gate event's correlation_id) and to one PO line (po_ref_ext + line_no). A reversed gate entry cannot be weighed (code: WEIGHBRIDGE_BINDING_TOKEN_NOT_FOUND).
  23. DECISION Weights in kg, up to 3 decimals. Net = gross - tare; a negative net is refused (code: WEIGHBRIDGE_NET_NEGATIVE).
  24. DECISION Tolerance is checked on the NET weight against the PO line ordered quantity with its under/over receipt tolerance %, not against the challan weight (code: applyWeighbridgeProjection). Challan weight is shown for information only.
  25. DECISION Out of tolerance = Exception: receipt is blocked and a task goes to the receiving supervisor role (Mahesh Tyagi, same exceptions list as the floor mock).
  26. DECISION Capture method is recorded as AUTO (read from indicator) or MANUAL (typed). Code has no reason rule for manual, so no reason is asked.
  27. DECISION An overdue legal-metrology stamp on the weighbridge blocks weighing until re-stamped (code: 423 WEIGHBRIDGE_OUT_OF_STAMP).
  28. ASSUMPTION Gross at weigh in (loaded), tare at weigh out (empty) for inbound trucks. Code needs both in one weighbridge.recorded event; the mock holds the gross reading as "Captured - pending sync" until tare is taken.
  29. ASSUMPTION Offline, the tolerance result is provisional (checked against the synced PO line); the server check on sync is final.
  30. ASSUMPTION Indicator photo is optional evidence; not a field in code. Unloading and Unloading done are set by the unloading supervisor; the mock lets you tap them.
  31. ASSUMPTION Truck statuses (Entered - weigh in pending, Weighed in, Unloading, Weigh out pending, Weighed out - net recorded, Exception), gate entry numbers GE-0925-nnn, bridge WB-01 and PO tolerance -2% / +5% are illustrative.
  32. ASSUMPTION Nav items other than Gate and Trucks on site (More, other sidebar modules) are placeholders, not rendered here.
  33. ASSUMPTION Once submitted, an entry is locked; the step list is no longer clickable.
