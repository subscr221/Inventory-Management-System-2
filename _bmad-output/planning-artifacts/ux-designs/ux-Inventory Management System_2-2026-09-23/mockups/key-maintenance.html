Maintenance - technician worklist and supervisor board

One frame, one shared state. Pick a device class and a role: every role works on every device; the role decides what is shown, the device class decides how. The technician's device works offline and its captures reach the supervisor only after sync. Approvals (return to service, warranty override, conflict resolution) need a live connection. Main path: open WO-2026-0144, Start work, record a meter reading, scan spare lot L-2609-117 and confirm issue, close with codes, notes and photo, bring Arif's device online, then switch to Supervisor and sign return to service.

Legend - decisions rendered

  1. DECISION Every role on every device: technician and supervisor both work in Handheld, Tablet, Desktop and Desktop touch. Role decides what is shown; device class decides how.
  2. DECISION Handheld (390 x 800, finger): touch-roomy, primary 56px pinned at the bottom, secondary 48px, bottom nav, single column, a list item opens a detail page with a back button.
  3. DECISION Tablet (820 x 1180, finger): touch-roomy, icon rail with short labels on the left, list and detail panes.
  4. DECISION Desktop (1280 x 800, mouse): compact 34px controls, dark sidebar #1B2230 with Maintenance active, top bar with search, site switcher CMF-ALIGARH and + New, list and detail side by side. Desktop touch: same layout, touch-roomy density.
  5. DECISION Scanning: "Scanner ready" shows only on Handheld; Camera is offered on Handheld and Tablet; Type it is always available; Desktop says "USB scanner or type".
  6. DECISION Approvals need a live connection on every device: return-to-service signature, warranty override and conflict resolution are disabled while the supervisor's device is offline.
  7. DECISION Maintenance is one of the 8 role surfaces: maintenance_technician and maintenance_supervisor.
  8. DECISION Accent A Ledger Blue #1E6BC6 five-token family; --accent-on-dark marks the active nav item on the sidebar and rail. Lato first in the font stack.
  9. DECISION Zoho-style structure only: module sidebar, search, site switcher, + New in accent. No Zoho name, logo or icons.
  10. DECISION Offline-first: every technician capture, on any device, shows "Captured - pending sync"; the supervisor sees it only after sync.
  11. DECISION Stock on hold is not issuable: a spare lot on QC hold is blocked at scan and cannot be issued.
  12. DECISION maintenance_supervisor is an approver; the return-to-service signature is an approval (it would also appear in the Approvals inbox).
  13. ASSUMPTION Maintenance layouts were not elicited with the user; drafted from the existing edge components (worklist, status, meter reading, spares issue, closure, fault report).
  14. ASSUMPTION On Handheld and Tablet the supervisor's 5-column board becomes a list grouped by the same columns (a kanban does not fit); the kanban stays on Desktop and Desktop touch.
  15. ASSUMPTION Triage changes (priority, assign, create work order) are also disabled while the supervisor's device is offline; only approvals were ruled on.
  16. ASSUMPTION Status steps Accepted, In progress, Waiting spares, Done. Waiting spares maps to the existing on_hold status plus a note. Supervisor assignment arrives as "Assigned - accept".
  17. ASSUMPTION Closure keeps the existing three required codes (fault, cause, remedy) as tiles, adds notes and a photo; photo required on breakdown work orders. Code lists are mock values.
  18. ASSUMPTION Spares are issued by scanning the lot or bin label against the work order reservation (the existing component picks a reservation from a list).
  19. ASSUMPTION Asset stays out of service until the supervisor signs return to service after a 3-item checklist.
  20. ASSUMPTION Warranty override: on a warranty-flagged work order the supervisor either calls the OEM or overrides to repair in-house with a mandatory reason.
  21. ASSUMPTION Sync conflict example: an offline meter reading lower than the server reading; supervisor keeps server, keeps both or sends back.
  22. ASSUMPTION Priority scale P1 to P4 with text labels; other technicians shown by role title. Report fault from an asset scan pre-fills the asset tag; photo is optional. The camera viewfinder is not rendered.
