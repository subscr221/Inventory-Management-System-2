My requisitions - every employee, own signed-in device

The base 'employee' hat that every signed-in person has: raise a requisition, check stock, report damage, follow their own requests and see their own standing approvals. Every requisition goes to the head of department unless the person holds a standing approval or a self-approval limit. There are no shared terminals and no badge or PIN step: each person uses their own device. Switch the device class and the person below; the app state is kept across device switches.

Legend - decisions and assumptions

  1. DECISION Every role on every device: the role decides WHAT is shown, the device class decides HOW. Handheld = bottom nav, single column, list opens a detail page with Back. Tablet = icon rail, list and detail side by side. Desktop = dark sidebar, top bar with search, site switcher and + New, compact 30-36px controls. Desktop touch = desktop layout at touch-roomy size.
  2. DECISION Touch classes: primary action 56px, full width, pinned at the bottom; secondary 48px. "Scanner ready" shows only on Handheld; every scan also offers camera (Handheld, Tablet) and always "Type it"; Desktop says "USB scanner or type".
  3. DECISION No shared machine terminals: everyone, including machine operators, uses their own signed-in device. No badge or PIN step.
  4. DECISION Any employee can raise a requisition and check stock availability at any time. By default every requisition is approved by the requester's head of department, by value band. There is no grade or seniority list.
  5. DECISION Exception 1 - STANDING APPROVAL (ref SA-YYYY-NNN): a grant linking one person to an item or item group, e.g. SA-2026-014 Cleaning gear for Ramu. Optional monthly quantity cap and end date; quantity over the cap falls back to normal approval.
  6. DECISION Exception 2 - per-person "Self-approval limit Rs 25,000" (senior engineer): self-approves requisitions within the limit. Overrides backend SOD-01 for this case; needs a backend change.
  7. DECISION Grants and limits are assigned by the site head or head of department, need a one-time finance head approval, are revoked the same day the sign-in account is disabled, and can be revoked by the assigner any time. Assigners get pruning lists (default every 30 days); grants unused 90 days are flagged.
  8. DECISION The stores screen and issue slip state the grant, e.g. "Standing approval SA-2026-014 for Ramu (Cleaning gear). Storekeeper please issue."
  9. DECISION Each person sees their own grants, caps used and end dates under "My standing approvals" (Profile).
  10. DECISION Report damage is universal. The reporter only reports: the reported units go on hold (not issuable) and QC is informed. QC and finance decide together; if they disagree, the CEO decides.
  11. DECISION Hold scope: a report holds only the reported units; a lot-wide hold is an option for a quality issue.
  12. DECISION Report damage + Request replacement is one flow, in pilot scope; the replacement is a linked requisition.
  13. DECISION Approvals need a live connection (approver side). Requests and damage reports can be captured offline and show "Captured - pending sync".
  14. DECISION Accent A Ledger Blue #1E6BC6 token family; dark sidebar #1B2230; Zoho-style structure without Zoho name, logo or icons.
  15. ASSUMPTION Demo grants: Ramu - SA-2026-014 Cleaning gear (floor mop, phenyl 5 L, nitrile gloves, garbage bags), phenyl capped at 6 cans per month (4 used), ends 31 Mar 2027. Line 2 technician - SA-2026-023 Welding gloves, cap 8 pairs per month, ends 31 Dec 2026. Senior engineer - no grants, Self-approval limit Rs 25,000 per request.
  16. ASSUMPTION Value bands: up to Rs 1,00,000 the head of department approves; above that the finance controller. Pilot backend has one unbounded band.
  17. ASSUMPTION A requisition mixing routes is split into one request per route, so standing-approval lines are not held up by an approval.
  18. ASSUMPTION Standing-approval items are counter stock, so they are "Ready to collect" as soon as the request syncs. Stores counter hours 08:00 to 20:00.
  19. ASSUMPTION Estimated value is shown only to people with a self-approval limit, because the limit depends on it.
  20. ASSUMPTION Cost centre and purpose are prefilled from the person's department and can be changed.
  21. ASSUMPTION "Suspect whole lot" is a request only; the QC head decides the lot-wide hold. Damage reasons: Dead on arrival - electronic, Damaged component, Wrong item or spec, Other (photo + one line required).
  22. ASSUMPTION The replacement requisition follows the same approval rules as any requisition (Ramu's phenyl replacement is covered by their standing approval; a technician's PCB board needs the head of department).
  23. ASSUMPTION "Material issued to me" lists open issues to the person from the last 7 days; qty affected is capped at the qty issued.
