Legend - decisions rendered

  1. DECISION Every role on every device: role decides WHAT is shown, the device class decides HOW. No shared machine terminals. One shared state across device switches.
  2. DECISION Handheld (scanner or phone, 390 x 800): touch-roomy (primary 56px pinned in the bottom third, secondary 48px), bottom nav (Inbox, Scan, Stock, More), single column, list opens a detail page with Back.
  3. DECISION Tablet (820 x 1180): touch-roomy, icon rail on the left, inbox list and detail side by side.
  4. DECISION Desktop (1280 x 800): compact, dark Zoho-style sidebar #1B2230, top bar with search, site switcher CMF-ALIGARH and "+ New", list and detail side by side.
  5. DECISION Desktop touch (touchscreen PC): desktop layout with dark sidebar, touch-roomy density.
  6. DECISION Scanning: "Scanner ready" (hardware trigger) shows only on Handheld; Handheld and Tablet also offer Camera; every class always offers "Type it"; desktop reads "USB scanner or type".
  7. DECISION Accent A Ledger Blue #1E6BC6 with its five-token family; --accent-on-dark marks the active nav item on the sidebar and icon rail.
  8. DECISION Storekeeper job: accept material, photograph, assign rack, keep traceability, issue, shelve returns.
  9. DECISION Stores home = task inbox (Arrived - awaiting acceptance; Cleared by QC - put away; Issues pending; Returns to shelve) with an always-on-top Scan anything bar.
  10. DECISION All received items land first in the inspection bay. Acceptance assigns no rack; it shows "Destination: Inspection bay IB-01".
  11. DECISION Directed putaway happens after QC clearance, from the "Cleared by QC - put away" inbox group: the system suggests the rack, "Use suggested" or "Choose another rack".
  12. DECISION Racks carry barcode/QR labels; scanning the rack label confirms putaway.
  13. ASSUMPTION One-tap override: "Choose another rack" (list or scan). Override teaching the system is not shown.
  14. DECISION Storekeeper does not judge damage. Each line has an optional "Inspect on opening" flag (damage suspected or observed, optional photo): a note for the inspector, not a verdict.
  15. DECISION Excess (pilot): accepted qty is capped at ordered x (1 + tolerance) minus already received; the rest is recorded as "Excess - returned with vehicle" and not posted. 5% is a mock value.
  16. DECISION Short reasons are fixed tiles: Supplier short-shipped; Part delivery - balance to follow; Count mismatch with challan; Other (photo + one line required).
  17. DECISION Report damage is a universal action on any scanned lot/item (Issue, Return, Move, Report damage). It places stock on hold and sends it to QC; QC and finance concur on the outcome, CEO decides if they disagree.
  18. DECISION Report damage + Request replacement is one flow, in pilot scope (stub here).
  19. ASSUMPTION Report damage = lot quality hold + QC task with reason code and photo, reusing the QC module; finance decision surface still to be designed.
  20. ASSUMPTION Gate hands off to the stores inbox with challan, supplier, PO and gate photo; nothing retyped.
  21. DECISION Label printing is non-blocking: GRN completes, labels queue with Reprint per item; each QR resolves to GRN, PO line and current location.
  22. DECISION Unlisted supplier from gate appears in the inbox flagged for stores to match.
  23. DECISION Offline-first: completion shows "Captured - pending sync" and "Sent to inspection bay IB-01".
  24. ASSUMPTION Desktop control height stays at 30-36px (earlier 44px desk floor was never confirmed).
  25. ASSUMPTION Icon rail labels mirror the handheld bottom nav; "Type it" focuses the scan field; the camera viewfinder is not drawn (camera confirms a putaway rack, otherwise use Demo controls).
  26. DECISION On Handheld the primary action is pinned in the bottom third on every task screen: acceptance, putaway, scan results, report damage and issue.
  27. DECISION "Issues pending" opens the issue screen: pending requisitions with how each was approved. Issue = scan item/lot (camera or hardware trigger on Handheld, camera on Tablet, USB scanner on desktop, always Type it), quantity, confirm.
  28. DECISION Standing approval (ref SA-YYYY-NNN) is stated explicitly on the stores screen and repeated on the issue slip: "Standing approval SA-2026-014 for Ramu (Cleaning gear). Storekeeper please issue." Grants may carry a monthly cap; over the cap falls back to normal approval.
  29. DECISION Requisitions without a grant are approved by the head of department (shown as "Approved by Head of department"). Senior staff may self-approve within a per-person limit ("Within self-approval limit").
  30. DECISION A lot on QC hold cannot be issued: the scan is blocked with the reason and the lot to pick instead.
  31. DECISION Offline-first issue: completion reads "Issued - Captured - pending sync".
  32. ASSUMPTION Standing-approval cap counts quantity issued ("4 of 6 cans used this month"). "Senior engineer" is a display label for the self-approval holder, not a grade.
  33. ASSUMPTION Issue slip number ISS-2026-NNNN, one lot per line, issued qty may be reduced below requested but not raised; slip layout is illustrative.
  34. ASSUMPTION Ramu is a housekeeping employee name used only for the standing-approval example given by the user.
