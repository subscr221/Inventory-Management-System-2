Approvals inbox - DOA approvals and damage case concurrence

Every approver role works on every device class. Pick the device class above the frame and the approver role in Demo controls; the app state (role, open item, typed reason, concurrence) carries across switches. Each role sees only its own approval types. The damage case DMG-2026-0027 appears for both QC head and Finance controller; the requisition IND-2026-0461 appears for the Department head (the requester's Head of department, by value band). Standing approval and self-approval limit requests appear for the Finance controller (finance department head) for one-time approval.

  1. DECISION Every role on every device: any approver can decide on a handheld, tablet, desktop or touchscreen PC. Role decides WHAT is shown; the device class decides HOW. No shared machine terminals.
  2. DECISION Handheld (390 x 800, finger): touch-roomy, bottom nav, single column; the list opens a detail page with a back button and a pinned footer (Approve 56px full width, Reject and Ask for info 48px).
  3. DECISION Tablet (820 x 1180 at 55%, finger): touch-roomy, icon rail on the left, list and detail as two panes.
  4. DECISION Desktop (1280 x 800 at 70%, mouse): compact 30-36px controls, dark sidebar #1B2230, top bar with search, site switcher CMF-ALIGARH and + New, list and detail side by side.
  5. DECISION Desktop touch: the desktop layout with dark sidebar, but touch-roomy targets.
  6. DECISION Approvals need a live connection on every device and are never queued offline. Offline shows a block message; evidence stays readable from the last sync.
  7. DECISION Approvals inbox is one surface for department head, site head (warehouse manager), QC head, maintenance supervisor, finance controller and CFO.
  8. DECISION Accent A Ledger Blue #1E6BC6 with its five-token family; --accent-on-dark marks the active Approvals nav item.
  9. DECISION Damage outcome needs concurrence of BOTH QC and finance, in either order; it locks only when both have concurred.
  10. DECISION If QC and finance disagree, the case escalates to the CEO for the final decision.
  11. DECISION Anyone can report damage, incl. machine operators from their own signed-in phone at the machine; reporting places stock on hold.
  12. DECISION Hold scope: local damage holds only the reported units; a quality issue can hold all remaining stock of the lot everywhere.
  13. DECISION Report damage + Request replacement is one flow in pilot scope (replacement indent shown in the case history).
  14. DECISION Any employee can raise a requisition and check stock. Requisitions without a standing approval go to the requester's Head of department by value band (IND-2026-0461).
  15. DECISION Standing approval (SA-YYYY-NNN) links a person to an item group, optionally with a monthly cap and end date; assigned by the site head or Head of department; needs a one-time approval by the finance department head (Finance controller) before it takes effect (SA-2026-021).
  16. DECISION Self-approval limit is set per person with the same assign / finance-head approve / review cycle (Rs 25,000 example). Detail shows who asked, items or limit, cap, end date and the person's last 90 days usage; actions are Approve (one-time) or Reject with reason.
  17. DECISION Grants are revoked the same day the person's sign-in is disabled; assigners and approvers get pruning lists (default every 30 days).
